# Public Research Notes For Anonymous network operator / RTGS-Inspired Scenarios

These notes summarize the public product categories used to inspire scenarios `06` through `10`. They are not internal Anonymous network operator, bank, or central-bank architecture claims.

## Public Sources Consulted

- Anonymous QR payment rail pages describe QR payments using one QR across participating banks and e-wallets.
- Anonymous network operator Direct Bank Gateway pages describe online banking payments, checkout redirection, real-time debit, and merchant confirmation.
- Anonymous network operator Biller Presentment pages describe online bill payments across participating banks and billers.
- Anonymous network operator Domestic Debit and Shared ATM material describe domestic debit acceptance and interbank ATM switching infrastructure.
- Central Bank Operator Wholesale Settlement Rail operational material describes Wholesale Settlement Rail as a real-time gross settlement system for interbank funds transfer and securities settlement.

## Scenario Design Principle

The artifacts use public product concepts only to create synthetic PQC scan material. They intentionally include parser-friendly cryptographic terms and exposure context so the scanner can test graph extraction, exposure-aware risk, remediation priority, and evidence traceability.

## Safety Boundary

No artifact contains real Anonymous network operator topology, production endpoint names, bank credentials, account numbers, PINs, API keys, private keys, customer data, or live IP addresses.
