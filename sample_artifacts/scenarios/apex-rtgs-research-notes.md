# Public Research Notes For ApexNet / RTGS-Inspired Scenarios

These notes summarize the public product categories used to inspire scenarios `06` through `10`. They are not internal ApexNet, bank, or central-bank architecture claims.

## Public Sources Consulted

- ApexNet DuitInstant QR pages describe QR payments using one QR across participating banks and e-wallets.
- ApexNet DirectPay pages describe online banking payments, checkout redirection, real-time debit, and merchant confirmation.
- ApexNet BillPay pages describe online bill payments across participating banks and billers.
- ApexNet MyDebit and Shared ATM material describe domestic debit acceptance and interbank ATM switching infrastructure.
- Bank Negara Malaysia ApexRTGS operational material describes ApexRTGS as a real-time gross settlement system for interbank funds transfer and securities settlement.

## Scenario Design Principle

The artifacts use public product concepts only to create synthetic PQC scan material. They intentionally include parser-friendly cryptographic terms and exposure context so AETHER can test graph extraction, exposure-aware risk, remediation priority, and evidence traceability.

## Safety Boundary

No artifact contains real ApexNet topology, production endpoint names, bank credentials, account numbers, PINs, API keys, private keys, customer data, or live IP addresses.
